PRODUCT INFORMATION

Card count by set

Three sets are currently planned: Knight, Mage, and Elf. Each set contains 52 cards: 1 character, 1 territory, 30 skills, and 20 items.

The Knight focuses on melee attacks and defence, the Mage on ranged magic, and the Elf on ranged physical attacks, movement, and healing. Card counts are per set.
