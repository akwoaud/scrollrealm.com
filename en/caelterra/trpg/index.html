Campaigns, Characters, and Realm-Shaping Choices

Caelterra TRPG uses the same world as the TCG to create a campaign-ready tabletop roleplaying experience. Players create heroes, explore ancient lands, negotiate with factions, and confront forces that can reshape the realm.
