About the game

Caelterra is a tabletop roleplaying game (TRPG) built on the deckbuilding and card play of a trading card game (TCG).

Build a deck and develop your character

Choose a character and territory, then build your deck with skill and item cards. Move across the map, fight encounters, and complete quests to develop your character.
