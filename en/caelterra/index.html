TWO WAYS TO PLAY

Strategy. Storytelling.

Caelterra is Scrollrealm’s flagship fantasy universe, designed to support both competitive collectible card play and narrative tabletop roleplaying. The TCG focuses on faction identity, deck construction, and tactical combat. The TRPG expands the same world into campaigns, characters, mysteries, and realm-changing stories.
