GAME OVERVIEW

A little farm.
A surprising numbers game.

Reveal cards, predict today’s farm and collect sunflower seeds. Every new card can change the outcome.

READ THE CARDS

Hamsters, kittens and a farm

The farm card sets the number to compare. Add the hamsters, subtract the kittens and see how many hamsters remain.

MAKE YOUR PREDICTION

A seed on your prediction

Place one sunflower seed on the outcome you expect before all the animal cards are revealed. Collect seeds for correct predictions.

HOW TO PLAY

One round, three steps

01

Reveal cards

Reveal one farm card and two animal cards.

02

Make a prediction

Place one sunflower seed on your predicted outcome.

03

Check the result

Reveal two more animal cards and calculate the hamsters remaining.
