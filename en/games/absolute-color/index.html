GAME OVERVIEW

Simple shapes.
A new world of color.

절대색감 is a puzzle built around overlapping transparent cyan, magenta and yellow pieces. Explore how familiar shapes and colors change as you layer them.

LAYER THE COLORS

Three colors, more possibilities

Transparent cyan, magenta and yellow pieces create new colors where they overlap. Look through the layers and explore the combinations.

EXPLORE THE SHAPES

A new view of familiar shapes

Combine circles, squares, triangles and other geometric pieces. Their position and overlap change the pattern you see.

HOW TO PLAY

See it. Layer it. Compare it.

01

Look at the target

Look closely at the colors and shapes in the target pattern.

02

Layer the pieces

Choose transparent pieces and explore their position and overlaps.

03

Compare the result

Compare the colors and shapes you have made with the target pattern.
