About Scrollrealm

Scrollrealm is the tabletop game studio behind Caelterra. We design and develop roleplaying games, card games, and puzzles.

Our games

  • Caelterra

    Build a deck from character, domain, skill, and item cards and take it on a tabletop roleplaying adventure.

  • Hamster Commotion

    Read the animal cards and predict how many hamsters will be left on the farm.

  • 절대색감

    Layer transparent coloured pieces to match the colours and shapes on a target card.

What we’re working toward

We want players meeting for the first time to be able to start a game together, and to find new choices when they come back. We design around the decisions players make at the table, from choosing a card for a deck to finding where a transparent piece fits.

With Caelterra, we want to build a world players can return to as their characters grow from one adventure to the next. We also want to keep making card games and puzzles that bring together people with different tastes.
